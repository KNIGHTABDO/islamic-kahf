# Chapter 1 fact sheet: أصحاب الكهف (الفصل الأول)

Scope of chapter 1: Surat al-Kahf 18:9-18 (the youths' faith, the stand before the king, the decision to leave, the cave,
the sleep, the sun, the turning, the dog, the awe). Chapter 2 starts at 18:19 (the awakening and the coin), so the sleep
must NOT be shown ending here. 18:12 ("then We raised them") stays: it is the Qur'an's own summary in 18:9-12; the narration
only hints that an awakening will come, without telling it. Cliffhanger: they sleep on, and nobody yet knows for how long.

Files: `sources/ch01/ayat_18_9-26.txt` (Uthmani text), `sources/ch01/tafsir_ibn_kathir.txt`, `tafsir_saadi.txt`,
`tafsir_muyassar.txt`, `tafsir_tabari.txt` (quran.com API), `sources/ch01/hadith.md` (checked on sunnah.com 2026-11-14).

Recitation lengths (Yasser Ad-Dussary): 18:9 8s, 18:10 22s, 18:11 7s, 18:12 10s, 18:13 12s, 18:14 22s, 18:15 22s,
18:16 21s, 18:17 29s, 18:18 26s. All ten = ~3 min.

## A. Firm facts (Qur'an and sahih hadith). Lines tagged [gloss] are tafsir: attribute them, never state them flat.
1. Allah tells the Prophet ﷺ: the story of the people of the cave and al-Raqim is not the most wondrous of His signs (18:9).
   Ibn Kathir: the creation of the heavens and earth, night and day, sun and moon are greater wonders. (Ibn Kathir 18:9, Mujahid)
2. They were "fitya" (فتية): young men (18:10, 18:13). Ibn Kathir: youth accept the truth more readily than elders hardened in falsehood.
3. When they took refuge in the cave they prayed: "Our Lord, grant us mercy from Yourself and set right our affair" (18:10).
   Ibn Kathir: they fled with their religion from their people so they would not be turned from it.
4. Allah "struck upon their ears" in the cave for a number of years: He cast sleep on them (18:11).
5. Then He raised them to make clear which of the two groups better counted how long they stayed (18:12). (Detail is chapter 2.)
6. They believed in their Lord and Allah increased them in guidance (18:13).
7. Allah "bound their hearts" (made them firm) when they STOOD UP and said: our Lord is the Lord of the heavens and earth; we
   will never call on any god besides Him, or we would have said an outrageous thing (18:14). They spoke this openly.
8. They said: these people of ours took gods besides Him; why don't they bring a clear proof? Who is more unjust than one who
   invents a lie about Allah? (18:15)
9. They said to one another: since you have left them and what they worship except Allah, take refuge in the cave; your Lord
   will spread His mercy over you and prepare ease for you in your affair (18:16).
10. The sun, when it rose, inclined away from their cave to the right; when it set, it passed them by on the left, while they
    lay in an open space inside it. That is one of Allah's signs (18:17). [gloss, Ibn Kathir / Ibn Abbas]: so the sun did not touch
    and burn them, yet light and air entered. [Ibn Kathir's own deduction, say "ذكر بعض المفسرين" or skip]: the cave mouth faced north.
11. You would think them awake while they were asleep (18:18). [tafsir gloss, say "ذكر بعض أهل العلم"]: their eyes stayed open.
12. Allah turned them to the right and to the left (18:18). [gloss, say "قال ابن عباس"]: had they not been turned, the earth would have consumed them.
    [gloss, say "قال بعض السلف"]: turned twice a year.
13. Their dog lay stretching its forelegs at the threshold (الوصيد = the entrance/courtyard) (18:18). [gloss, Ibn Kathir / Ibn Jurayj]: it lay at
    the door as dogs do, as if guarding; the dog shared their blessing and their sleep: "this is the benefit of good company".
14. Had you looked upon them you would have turned away fleeing, filled with terror (18:18). [gloss, Ibn Kathir]: Allah clothed them
    in awe so no one would approach or touch them until the appointed term.
15. Allah did NOT tell us where the cave is, because there is no religious benefit in it (Ibn Kathir 18:17).
16. Number (for the hook only, details come in chapter 3): the Qur'an says "say: my Lord knows best their number" (18:22).
    Ibn Abbas said "I am of the few who know them: they were seven" (Ibn Kathir: sahih chains to Ibn Abbas). Present as
    "قال ابن عباس".
17. Hadith (sahih): "من حفظ عشر آيات من أول سورة الكهف عُصم من الدجال" (Muslim 809, Abu al-Darda'). Optional for the opening.
18. Hadith (sahih): "يوشك أن يكون خير مال المسلم غنم يتبع بها شعف الجبال ومواقع القطر يفر بدينه من الفتن" (Bukhari 19,
    Abu Sa'id al-Khudri). Ibn Kathir cites it at 18:15: fleeing to keep one's religion in times of fitna. Optional.
19. Hadith (sahih): "الأرواح جنود مجندة، فما تعارف منها ائتلف، وما تناكر منها اختلف" (Bukhari 3336 from Aisha, Muslim 2638 from
    Abu Hurayra). Ibn Kathir uses it for how the youths found each other. Optional.

## B. Reported details (قيل / يُروى / ذكر المفسرون): never present as certain
20. يُروى عن ابن عباس (cited by Ibn Kathir 18:13, "وقد تقدم عن ابن عباس"): Quraysh sent to the rabbis of the Jews in Madina asking
    for things to test the Prophet ﷺ with; the rabbis told them to ask him about these youths, about Dhul-Qarnayn, and about
    the Ruh. Ibn Kathir: so their story was preserved in the books of the People of the Book. ONLY this much; no wording of
    the question or of the rabbis beyond it. Usable as the cold open, said with "يُروى".
21. ذكر غير واحد من المفسرين (Ibn Kathir 18:14): they were sons of the nobles/kings of the Rum; their people worshipped idols and
    sacrificed to them at a yearly festival outside the city; their king was a stubborn tyrant called "دقيانوس" (said with
    "يُقال"), who ordered people to it.
22. Ibn Kathir 18:14 (reported): at the festival each youth, seeing the prostration to idols, slipped away alone; one sat in the
    shade of a tree, another came and sat by him, then another, none knowing the others, until one said: something made each
    of you leave, let each reveal his matter; each confessed he believed in the One God; they became brothers and took a
    place to worship Allah. Their people informed the king; he summoned them; they answered with the truth (this is where
    18:14 "إذ قاموا" fits).
23. فيُقال (Ibn Kathir 18:15): the king refused, threatened them, ordered their fine clothes (the adornment of their people)
    stripped from them, and gave them time to reconsider. In that respite they fled.
24. يُقال (Ibn Kathir 18:16): the king searched for them and did not find them; Allah hid their news from him.
    (Another report that the king had the cave sealed on them: Ibn Kathir rejects it, "وفي هذا نظر". Do NOT use.)
25. قيل (Ibn Kathir 18:18): the dog was a hunting dog of one of them ("وهو الأشبه"); another report: it belonged to the king's
    cook who joined them. Its name: Qitmir (al-Hasan al-Basri via Ibn Asakir) or Humran (Shu'ayb al-Jaba'i). Ibn Kathir doubts
    the names (mostly from the People of the Book). If used, only as "قيل" and once. Its colour: do NOT mention
    (Ibn Kathir: guessing at the unseen).
26. Ibn Kathir 18:13: some said they followed the religion of Isa (AS); Ibn Kathir thinks they were before Christianity.
    Scholars differ: say "والله أعلم" or avoid the era. Do not name a century.
27. Mujahid (Ibn Kathir 18:13): "بلغني" some of them wore earrings (sign of wealth). Optional, as "قيل".
28. Place: Ibn Abbas: near Ayla (Aqaba); Ibn Ishaq: near Nineveh; others: lands of the Rum; others: al-Balqa' (Jordan).
    Ibn Kathir: Allah knows in which land. Say the place is unknown; NEVER name a city as certain.
29. Al-Raqim: a tablet/inscription (most likely, Ibn Jarir's choice: a written record, "مرقوم"), or the valley, or the
    mountain. Mention as a difference of opinion if at all.
30. Ibn Kathir on 18:16 compares their cave with the cave of Thawr, where Allah hid the Prophet ﷺ and Abu Bakr (RA) from
    Quraysh Optional link; DO NOT quote the hadith wording (not re-checked); the Prophet ﷺ is never shown.

## C. Not for chapter 1
- How long they slept (300 + 9 years, 18:25), the awakening, the coin, the discovery, the number debate (18:19-26). Hint only.
- Names of the youths (Ibn Kathir doubts them). Do not use.
